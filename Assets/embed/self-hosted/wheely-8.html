Game Overview

Wheely 8 Web is a browser-based game where you control a vehicle as it navigates through various levels, collecting coins and avoiding obstacles while trying to reach the finish line. The game has a unique blend of driving mechanics and platformer elements, making it an entertaining and challenging experience for players.

How to Play

The controls are simple: use the arrow keys or mouse to steer your vehicle left or right, and press space to jump. The objective is to collect as many coins as possible while avoiding hazards like spikes, pitfalls, and other obstacles. In each level, you'll find a series of checkpoints where you can earn bonus points for collecting all the coins in a specific order. Completing levels quickly will also earn you extra points. However, be careful not to touch any hazards, or your vehicle will take damage and have to restart from the last checkpoint.

This will help you anticipate where obstacles are likely to be and plan your route accordingly. * Use the right steering input: If your vehicle is on fire, use the left arrow key to slow down or the right arrow key to accelerate. If it's too hot, hold the mouse button to cancel the jump. * Avoid obstacles head-on: When possible, try to avoid touching hazards by steering around them instead of directly heading into the middle. * Practice jumps and boosts: Timing your jumps and boosts perfectly is crucial to collecting coins efficiently. * Keep an eye on your health: If your vehicle takes damage, you'll have to restart from a checkpoint. This means that if you're running low on health, it's better to focus on collecting coins rather than trying to complete the level.
